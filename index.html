Frequently Asked Questions

Are these leads already claimed by someone else?

Every lead is verified unclaimed within 30 days before delivery. Cases under 90 days old have a < 15% contacted rate. You're calling before your competitors — that's the whole point.

What exactly is in the CSV each Monday?

Owner full name, county, state, case number, surplus amount, claim deadline (exact date), lead score (0–100), property address. Growth plan adds verified phone + current address.

Which counties do you cover exactly?

FL: Hillsborough, Pinellas, Palm Beach, Broward, Miami-Dade, Orange · TX: Harris, Dallas, Tarrant, Bexar, Travis · GA: Fulton, DeKalb, Gwinnett, Cobb · OH: Franklin, Cuyahoga, Hamilton, Summit · NC/PA/CA: top 4–6 counties by volume. Your target county not listed? Email us — new counties are added monthly on request.

Can I choose my specific county?

Yes. At signup, reply with your target state and preferred counties. Your county is prioritized in the weekly batch. If you're on Own Bot, you configure your own county list with no limits.

PropStream already gives me foreclosure data — what's different?

PropStream tracks pre-foreclosures and distressed properties. SurplusBot tracks the surplus funds AFTER the foreclosure sale — money that belongs to the prior owner. These are different cases, different people, different opportunity. PropStream users are our best customers.

I have a VA already. Should I switch?

Many subscribers keep both. SurplusBot handles the sourcing and scoring — your VA handles outreach and paperwork. A good VA costs $400–800/mo; SurplusBot is $197–297. If your VA is also doing data sourcing, you're probably overpaying for that part.

What if I'm not satisfied with the leads?

If your first delivery has fewer than 30 workable leads (score 60+, open deadline), we credit your next month. No questions. See the full guarantee above.

Are the leads exclusive?

Not fully exclusive — we serve multiple subscribers. But cases are < 90 days old and non-contacted, so you're early enough to be first. Exclusivity per county is available on the Own Bot plan.
